# Update 93 - LINEDLE: the "Wave panel look" card is now easy to find

- The card was filed under the **Gameplay** tab, buried among other settings. It now lives under **Settings -> Look & sound -> Wave panel look** (with a 🌊 icon).
- NEW **🌊 button in the top bar**: one tap opens Settings straight on the Wave panel look card.
- The card holds: panel box (background, border, corners, height), pillars (show / hide, color, strength, width, outline, numbers under them), horizontal letter lines (show / hide, color, strength, thickness, style, which letters), letters on the left side (show / hide, color, strength, size, which letters), light-blue wave (color / Auto, thickness, glow, dots), white wave (color / Auto, thickness, glow, dots, letters in dots, faint trails) and 5 ready looks + reset.
- Files: `public/shared/settings-shell.js` (category + icon rule), `server/linedle/public/linedle-index.html`, `linedle-game.js`.
