# Update 121 - CONTEXTO gets Appearance + the 15 cute looks; every game gets the cute 3D layer

Based on update 120 (included).

## CONTEXTO
- The 🌙 toolbar button now opens a two-group Appearance menu: "Contexto colors" (the 7 you already had) and "🧸 Box colors & looks" (the same 15 designs as BOXDLE and every other game).
- A look recolors the page, panels, borders, text, buttons and accents, and adds the scrapbook decoration: graph-paper page, washi tape and stitched frame on the title, sparkles, raised 3D pill buttons, raised guess rows, rounded Fredoka/Quicksand fonts.
- The rank colors (gold / green / orange / red) are NOT changed by a look.
- The choice is shared: pick a look in CONTEXTO and every other game uses it; pick one anywhere else and CONTEXTO follows the next time it opens. Picking a Contexto color keeps working as before.
- Contexto's own theme attribute was renamed (data-theme -> data-ctx) so it no longer clashes with the platform theme.

## All other games (while a look is active)
Rounded cute fonts, glossy raised 3D tiles and keys, raised pill buttons, stitched dashed cards, twinkling sparkles on the title - on top of the colors and graph-paper/washi-tape of update 120. Clue colors are untouched. The 6 plain colors are unchanged.
