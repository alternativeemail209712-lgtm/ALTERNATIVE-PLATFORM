# Update 122 - cute title/header styles + 3D design for every game

Based on update 121 (included). Only 2 files changed: public/shared/theme.js, public/shared/theme.css (+ package.json 2.70.0). No game HTML needed editing.

## New: "Title & header style" (Settings -> Appearance, under the color list; shared by all games)
Works while one of the 15 cute looks is active, in every game with a title banner (BOXDLE and CONTEXTO keep their own).
- 🎀 Sticker (BOXDLE style) - default: two-tone sticker-outlined title, purple dashed ribbon with a per-game word (e.g. WORD GUESS), crown + twinkling sparkles, handwritten tagline, paper-strip top bar.
- 🍬 Candy 3D: puffy extruded letters, notched banner ribbon, tagline in a stitched pill, hearts.
- 📒 Notebook label: lined paper card with punch holes, washi tape, wavy underline title, pink sticker pill.
- 🖼️ Classic banner: the update-121 banner.
The title auto-shrinks to fit, and the existing "title font" picker still works on top of every style.

## More 3D / cute
- Top bar, buttons, badges, inputs: BOXDLE paper-strip look (dashed edges, raised rounded buttons).
- Main game box: stitched sticker card with washi tape and a 💗.
- Raised glossy 3D for tiles, keys and every tile-like piece (Expandle, Minedle, Codedle, Comparable, Shapedle, Structle, color swatches, chips, legend tiles, status/hint chips, answer rows). Clue colors are never changed.
