Starter word

Off (default - the blue wave is already the first clue): every round starts on a blank guess board. On: one random word is placed on the board for free at the start of each round (worth no points) so chat has first colors to read. Takes effect from the next round; your choice is remembered.

Strict fit

Off (default): any real word of the right length is accepted and colored, so chat can probe freely. On: a guess must also still fit the colors of every guess on the board, otherwise it is skipped with the message below. (A guess must ALWAYS be a real word of the right length.) Your choice is remembered and applies right away.

    How to play

    • Guess the hidden word — its length is chosen in Settings (4 to 20 letters). Unlimited guesses, anyone can play, anytime.
    • The board has pillars - one for every letter of the secret word (5 pillars = a 5-letter word). The light-blue wave-line runs through the pillars: the height where it crosses a pillar is the letter at that position.
    • The lower the line, the closer to A. The higher the line, the closer to Z (A at the bottom, Z at the very top - see the A to Z scale on the left).
    • Chat guesses real words of the right length. Every guess is drawn as a white wave-line with the same rule. The round is won when the white line sits exactly on the blue line.
    • After every guess each letter turns Green, Yellow or Grey:
      Green — correct letter, right position.
      Yellow — the letter is in the word but in the wrong position.
      Grey — the letter is not in the word.
    • A word already on the board is skipped. Strict fit is off by default; the host can switch it on in Settings so only guesses that still fit every earlier color are accepted.
    • Scoring (Live): 1 point for the guess that solves the round.
    • 💡 unlimited hints suggest a word that fits the clues so far (never the answer). Difficulty (Normal/Medium/Hard/Random) sets how tricky the word itself is.
    • Live = real TikTok chat. Test = simulated chat, nothing saved. Offline = just you, type guesses directly.
