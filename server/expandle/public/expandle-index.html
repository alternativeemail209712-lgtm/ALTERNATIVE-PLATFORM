Connects to your LIVE room and feeds real chat guesses straight into the game. Only used in Live mode.

Round time

How long chat has to grow the word in every round (default 60 seconds, 10 to 600). The clock starts when the round starts and the round ends when it reaches zero, when the word can not grow any further, or when you tap Give up. Takes effect when you tap Apply.

Start word length

How long the free first word of every round is. A longer start means fewer rows before the word can not grow any more. Takes effect when you tap Apply; your choice is remembered.

Maximum word length

The round ends as soon as a word reaches this length (a short cap makes rounds quicker). Automatic = the longest word the dictionary has. Takes effect when you tap Apply; your choice is remembered.

Difficulty

Decides how the start word of every round is picked, by how far it can grow. Takes effect when you tap Apply; your choice is remembered.

Colors

Every letter tile has the same color. Pick the tile color, the letter color, the tile border, the frame of the newest row and the clock. Every change applies right away on every connected screen and is remembered. The legend always shows the same colors - change a color here or on a legend chip and the other follows.

Win celebration

After a win, the leaderboard pops up automatically, then closes on its own. Auto-continue (above) always waits for this celebration to finish before starting the next round.

Floating message window

    How to play

    • Every round starts with a short start word (3 letters by default; the host can change it - the first row, free). The goal: make the word grow, one letter per row.
    • To make the next row, shuffle all the letters of the newest word and add exactly ONE new letter (any letter) to make a longer real word. Example: ARE → TEAR (+T) → STARE (+S) → TRADES (+D)…
    • Row 2 has 4 letters, row 3 has 5 letters, row 4 has 6 letters… up to as many as you can reach. Only the first valid word for each row is accepted, then the next row builds on it. The newest row is always on top.
    • You have 60 seconds per round (the host can change it). Everyone can play, any number of words.
    • Scoring (Live): 1 point for every accepted word - 1 point per row you win.
    • All letter tiles share one color (the host can change it in Settings). Each row shows who made it, on the left. The legend above the board explains everything.
    • When time is up (or the word cannot grow any more) the round ends with the top scorer, the round leaderboard and the all-time leaderboard.
    • 💡 unlimited hints: a word that grows the newest row, a letter you can add, or letters that will NOT work.
    • Live = real TikTok chat. Test = simulated chat, nothing saved. Offline = type words yourself.
