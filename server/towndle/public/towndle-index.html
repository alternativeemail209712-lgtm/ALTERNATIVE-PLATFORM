Difficulty

Decides how famous the secret place is (Normal = famous + well-known, Medium = well-known + lesser-known, Hard = lesser-known and rare only, Random = every place of every tier). The list covers cities, municipals, districts and towns of all 13 states and the Federal Territories; chat can guess any place on it (with or without spaces, capitals or hyphens).

Secret place state

Tick the states the SECRET place may come from (at least one; 14 = 13 states + the Federal Territories as the 14th). Chat can still guess any place in Malaysia. Takes effect when you tap Apply; your choice is remembered.

Distance unit

Only changes how distances are shown on the board, in the floating message and in messages. It applies right away on every screen and is remembered.

Place colors

Customize every part of the game: for each of the four place tiles its tile color, text color, state-text color and border; the direction and distance badges; the frame around the newest guess and the ring around the winner; the column headings; and the shape and text (border width, roundness, row height, spacing, frame thickness, city-name size and boldness, country line size, show the country, capital letters). Every change applies right away on every connected screen and is remembered. The legend always shows the same colors - change a color here or on a legend chip and the other follows.

Win celebration

After a win, the leaderboard pops up automatically, then closes on its own. Auto-continue (above) always waits for this celebration to finish before starting the next round.

Starter place

On (default): one random well-known place is placed on the board for free at the start of each round (worth no points) so chat has a first color, direction and distance to read. Off: every round starts on a blank board. Takes effect at the next round.

Strict fit

Off (default): any place of the list is accepted, so chat can probe freely. On: a place must agree with EVERY guess already on the board - the same color and about the same distance (within 10%) as the secret city would have produced - otherwise it is skipped with a message in the floating window. The secret city itself always passes. Applies right away; your choice is remembered.

Floating message window

The small window above the legend and the board. It announces each new guess with its score and shows a short message whenever a guess is skipped (for example by Strict fit).

Difficulty, auto-continue and this delay only take effect once you tap Apply. Win-celebration duration applies right away.

    How to play

    • Guess the secret place in Malaysia. Unlimited guesses, anyone can play, anytime. Type any real city, municipal, district or town (big or small).
    • Every guess is colored: green = the right place, yellow = the right state but the wrong place (13 states + the Federal Territories as the 14th), orange = a direct neighbour of the secret place (same district or a touching district) but not the place itself, grey = wrong place, not a neighbour and not in the right state.
    • Each guess also shows an arrow (the direction to guess next, toward the secret place) and the distance (km or miles - the host's choice) from the secret place.
    • Guesses are listed from the nearest to the farthest place. The newest guess is repeated in a highlighted row at the very top (it turns green when the place is found).
    • A place already on the board is skipped. The small floating window above the legend announces every new guess.
    • The legend above the board explains every color and column (the host can customize or hide it).
    • Scoring (Live): the guess that finds the secret place wins 5 points. Nothing else scores.
    • 💡 unlimited hints: suggest a place that fits every clue on the board, reveal a letter, or rule out letters. Difficulty (Normal/Medium/Hard/Random) sets how famous the secret place is.
    • Live = real TikTok chat. Test = simulated chat, nothing saved. Offline = type guesses yourself.
