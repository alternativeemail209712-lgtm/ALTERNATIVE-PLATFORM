Difficulty

Scored on word familiarity, letter structure, and puzzle ambiguity — not just obscure vocabulary. "Random" skips scoring entirely and draws from every word of that length. Very short/long word lengths may not have every tier available.

Color shades

Choose how strong each board color (grey guess rows, dark newest-guess row, green found-word row) looks. Ten shades per color, from lightest to darkest (the middle one is the standard look). It applies right away - no new round needed - and every screen connected to this game changes together.

After a win, the leaderboard pops up automatically, then closes on its own. Auto-continue (above) always waits for this celebration to finish before starting the next round.

Starter word

On (default): one random word is placed on the board for free at the start of each round (worth no points) so chat has a first score to read. Off: every round starts on a blank board. Takes effect from the next round; your choice is remembered.

Floating message window

The small window above the legend and the board. It announces each new guess with its score and shows a short message whenever a guess is skipped.

    How to play

    • Guess the secret word. Unlimited guesses, anyone can play, anytime. Guesses can be any real word, any length.
    • Your score = matching prefix + matching suffix with the secret word. The prefix counts the letters that match from the start, the suffix counts the letters that match from the end. Example: secret SCRIPT, guess SCARRIEST → SC (2) + T (1) = 3.
    • Guesses are listed from the highest score to the lowest. The newest guess is repeated in the darker row at the top.
    • If your score reaches twice the word length, you found the exact secret word and win: the top row turns green. (A 6-letter word needs 12.)
    • A word already on the board is skipped. The small floating window above the legend announces every new guess with its score.
    • The legend above the board explains every part of the screen (the host can customize or hide it in Settings → Legends).
    • Scoring (Live): the guess that finds the secret word wins exactly the score of that secret word: twice its length (SCRIPT = 12 points, POLLUTION = 18 points).
    • 💡 unlimited hints: suggest a word that fits every score on the board, reveal a letter, or rule out letters. Difficulty (Normal/Medium/Hard/Random) sets how tricky the secret word is.
    • Live = real TikTok chat. Test = simulated chat, nothing saved. Offline = just you, type guesses directly.
