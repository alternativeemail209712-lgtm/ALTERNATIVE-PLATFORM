    Difficulty

    Scored on word familiarity, letter structure, and puzzle ambiguity — not just obscure vocabulary. "Random" skips scoring entirely and draws from every word of that length. Very short/long word lengths may not have every tier available.

    Color shades

    Choose how strong each clue color (white, pink and blue) looks, in the tiles, the legend and the keyboard. Ten shades per color, from lightest to darkest (the middle one is the standard look). It applies right away - no new round needed - and every screen connected to this game changes together.

    Color legend

    The legend sits under the floating message window and above the keyboard and the guess board. The second switch is OFF by default: the legend only shows the three colors with a "?" and chat has to work the meanings out from the clues, like the original Shuffl-O (they are always shown once the round has ended). Switch it ON if you want the legend to say what white, pink and blue mean in the current round. Applies right away on every connected screen and is remembered.

    Win celebration

    After a win, the leaderboard pops up automatically, then closes on its own. Auto-continue (above) always waits for this celebration to finish before starting the next round.

    Starter word

    On (default): one random word is placed on the board for free at the start of each round (worth no points) so chat has first colors to read. Off: every round starts on a blank board. Takes effect from the next round; your choice is remembered.

    Strict fit

    On (default): a guess must still fit the colors of every guess on the board - under ANY of the 6 ways the three meanings can be dealt to the three colors (like ORACLE), not only this round's real deal, so viewers who are still working out which color means what are not punished. Otherwise it is skipped with the message below. Off: any real word of the right length is accepted and colored, so chat can probe freely. Your choice is remembered and applies right away.

    Auto-color keyboard keys

    On (default): every key on the keyboard takes the same color as its letter's tiles on the board - the best clue wins (right spot, then wrong spot, then not in the word). Off: the keys stay plain (neutral) - viewers only read the colors on the tiles. Applies right away on every connected screen and is remembered.

    Guess rejection message

    Shown briefly whenever Strict fit skips a guess.

    Word length, difficulty, auto-continue and this delay only take effect once you tap Apply. Win-celebration duration applies right away.

      How to play

      • Guess the hidden word — any length you like, chosen in Settings. Unlimited guesses, anyone can play, anytime.
      • After every guess each letter turns White Pink or Blue. The three colors stand for three clues: right spot, in the word but the wrong spot, and not in the word.
      • But watch out — the meaning of the colors is shuffled every round! In one round blue may mean "right spot"; in the next, blue may mean "not in the word" and pink "right spot". Keep guessing and use the shifting colors to uncover the secret word.
      • The legend above the board shows the three colors and, when the host turns it on, what each color means in the current round (by default it only shows the three colors with a "?" and you work the meanings out from the clues; the host can switch on "Legend tells this round's meanings" to make it easier).
      • The keyboard colors itself to match the tiles — white, pink or blue — so there is nothing to mark by hand. Everything resets each new round.
      • A word already on the board is skipped. Strict fit is on by default, so only guesses that could still fit every earlier color are accepted - a guess counts as fitting if it works under any possible meaning of the colors, so you can test your own theory of which color means what; the host can switch it off in Settings.
      • Scoring (Live): 1 point for every guess that fits the board, 5 points for solving the round.
      • 💡 unlimited hints suggest a word that fits the clues so far (never the answer). Difficulty (Normal/Medium/Hard/Random) sets how tricky the word itself is.
      • Live = real TikTok chat. Test = simulated chat, nothing saved. Offline = just you, type guesses directly.
