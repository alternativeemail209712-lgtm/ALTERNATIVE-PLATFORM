    How to play

    • Guess the secret word. Unlimited guesses, anyone can play, anytime.
    • After each guess you see its distance from the secret word in alphabetical order. Example: if the distance is 120, there are 119 words between your guess and the secret word.
    • Your guess updates the lower bound (top row, a word that comes before the secret word) or the upper bound (bottom row, a word that comes after it), narrowing down the search. The secret word is always hidden in the ? row between them.
    • The smaller the distance, the closer you are. If the distance is 0, you found the secret word and the ? row turns green.
    • A guess that falls outside the two bounds is skipped, and a short "Word outside the allowed range" message pops up in the floating window. A word already on the board is skipped too.
    • The gold alphabet circles show the letters a guess can still start with (the letters from the lower bound to the upper bound); the rest are dimmed. The legend above the board explains them (the host can hide it in Settings).
    • Scoring (Live): 1 point for the guess that finds the secret word.
    • 💡 unlimited hints: suggest a word from the middle of the window, reveal a letter, or rule out letters. Difficulty (Normal/Medium/Hard/Random) sets how tricky the secret word is.
    • Live = real TikTok chat. Test = simulated chat, nothing saved. Offline = just you, type guesses directly.
