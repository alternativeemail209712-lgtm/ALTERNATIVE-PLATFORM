    TikTok auto-chat

    Connects to your LIVE room and feeds real chat guesses straight into the game. Only used in Live mode.

    Grid size

    Every round gets a new random grid of this size. Bigger grids hold more words but the tiles get smaller on a phone screen.

    Letters & tiles

    Off (default): every tile can be used only once in a word (classic word hunt). On: a word may come back to a tile it already used, so words with repeated letters like LEVEL or BANANA can be made from fewer tiles. A tile always has to touch the next one - up, down, sideways or corner.

    Round time

    Default: 1 minute (60 seconds). Choose any time from 10 seconds to 1 hour. The clock starts when the round starts; when it reaches zero the round ends and the player with the most points is celebrated.

    Color shades

    Choose how strong each of the special colors (the highlighted word path, valid words, the hint tile, the last-seconds red and the round clock) looks, in the grid, the board and the legend. Ten shades per color, from lightest to darkest (the middle one is the standard look). It applies right away - no new round needed - and every screen connected to this game changes together.

    Box colors & look

    Pick one of the 30 cute looks (the same ones as in the Appearance list above), or color every part yourself: the page, the largest box, its border and the three boxes inside it (clock, letter grid, guessed words), the tiles and their letters, the text, the pink accent and the navy title. Changes apply right away on every screen connected to this game and are remembered.

    Win celebration

    When a round ends, the winner and the leaderboards pop up automatically, then close on their own. Auto-continue (above) always waits for this celebration to finish before starting the next round.

    Floating message window

    Shows each new valid word (✓ with its points) and each skipped word (✗ with the reason) for a few seconds.

      How to play

      • The board is a grid of letter tiles — 6 × 6, 8 × 8, 10 × 10 or 12 × 12, chosen in Settings. Everyone sees the same grid.
      • Make words by chaining tiles that touch each other in every direction: up, down, left, right and the four corners. Type the word in the chat — anyone can play, anytime.
      • Only real words count (no made-up words). A word must be at least 4 letters (the host can change this) and can be as long as you can find — 15 or even 20 letters on the big grids and each word scores once per round — the first person to type it gets it.
      • Tiles: by default each tile can be used only once per word. The host can switch on Tiles may be reused, so a word may come back to a tile it already used.
      • Scoring: points = the number of letters. 5 a 5-letter word is 5 points, a 10-letter word is 10 points. Points count for the chat leaderboards in Live mode.
      • Each new word lights up its path on the grid (A gold tiles). Every valid word is listed in the Guessed words board with its points and who found it.
      • The round lasts 1 minute unless the host picks another time. When the clock hits zero the player with the most points wins the round and a new grid starts.
      • 💡 shows the first tile of a hidden word (A purple tile) and its length — never the word itself. Unlimited.
      • Live = real TikTok chat. Test = simulated chat, nothing saved. Offline = just you, type words directly.
